# Forecast backtest: VCE-like departures checkpoint, normal day (synthetic)

> SIMULATED RESULTS ON SYNTHETIC OR HYPOTHETICAL DATA. These numbers are model outputs, not measurements, and must not be presented as evidence of operational performance.

Scenario `vce-like-normal-day` (SYNTHETIC, `sha256:5658496bfd2d...`), policy `priority-v3`, 200 simulated days (seeds 1-200).

The truth is the full simulator's day. Every 15 minutes, from an hour after the first arrival, GateFlow forecasts from what the live system knows then: the arrivals of the last hour, the people not yet admitted, the lanes open. Arrivals come from 50 other simulated days of the scenario, scaled by the arrivals of the last hour; the lanes follow the scenario's plan, with today's difference from it.

MAE and bias are of the median (p50), bias above 0 when the forecast says more than happened; WAPE is the absolute errors over the truths. Calibrated bands hold 80 % of the truths between p10 and p90, with 10 % below and 10 % above; the narrower the band, the more it says. Most of the day nobody waits and none is forecast to: those forecasts are right but say nothing of the bands, so the people not yet admitted and the waits are also measured on the forecasts of a queue alone (median or truth at least 1).
